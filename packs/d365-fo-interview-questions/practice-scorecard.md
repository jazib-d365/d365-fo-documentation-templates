# Interview practice scorecard
## Answer structure
Explain the business situation, your responsibility, the configuration/data/process investigated, the decision, and the evidence of the result. If hypothetical, say so.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business outcome | Unclear | General goal | Specific measurable need |
| Assumptions | Unstated | Some mentioned | Relevant process/configuration stated |
| Investigation | Guess | Plausible checks | Ordered checks with evidence |
| Decision | Unsupported | Reason given | Options and tradeoffs explained |
| Validation | None | General testing | Acceptance, regression, and reconciliation |

Use the score to find weak areas, not to predict hiring outcomes.

## Five-session practice plan
1. Procurement and sales: explain a full flow and one partial quantity exception.
2. Inventory and warehouse: diagnose one work creation failure.
3. Migration: present a mapping and reconcile one rejected-row example.
4. Testing and integrations: design a permission test and a duplicate-message test.
5. Delivery: explain an incident, cutover risk, and business sign-off.
