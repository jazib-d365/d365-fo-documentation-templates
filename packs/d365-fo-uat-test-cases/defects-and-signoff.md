## Suggested decision gates
1. Every critical in-scope requirement has an executed acceptance scenario.
2. Blocking defects are resolved and retested.
3. Accepted residual risks have named business owners and documented mitigations.
4. End-to-end stock and financial reconciliation is reviewed by the appropriate owner.
5. Sign-off identifies the exact build and scope.

These are suggested controls, not universal Microsoft-mandated pass percentages.
